## About Me

I’m the CTO of TaoScope, a real-time analytics and infrastructure platform focused on the Bittensor ecosystem.

My work centers around designing and scaling distributed systems capable of processing high-throughput blockchain and network data in real time.

Over the years, I’ve worked across backend architecture, infrastructure engineering, analytics systems, and product development with a strong focus on reliability, scalability, and execution.

At TaoScope, we are building tools that help validators, miners, delegators, and researchers better understand and interact with decentralized AI networks.

### Areas of Focus

- Real-time analytics infrastructure
- Distributed systems & backend architecture
- Blockchain indexing & observability
- AI-integrated platforms
- Performance optimization & scalability
- Data pipelines & monitoring systems
